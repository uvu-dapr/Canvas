Final Project: Mac Navigation Deep Dive

This project pulls together the entire Mac Navigation unit - all five Parts - into one PDF you build yourself, demonstrating real fluency rather than just having opened the right windows once. Each item below covers several related concepts at once rather than testing one tiny fact in isolation.

Goals

  • Navigate confidently between apps and windows, and clear visual clutter with Command-Option-H.
  • Use Finder's Path Bar, Go to Folder, and Smart Folders as everyday navigation tools, not novelties.
  • Explain root vs. Home, and correctly identify /Library vs. ~/Library on your own Mac.
  • Identify what a file actually is - file, package, alias, or installer - and where it can safely be inspected.
  • Troubleshoot a frozen app, a resource-hungry process, or a missing driver approval without guessing.
  • Locate your own audio interface, its drivers, and its AAX plug-ins using the exact paths this unit covers.
