## House rules that apply to every prompt below

From Standards §20.5 and §20.5a:

- **Photorealistic is the house style.** Not flat vector, not line art, not clip art.
- **No text inside the image.** No titles, no axis names, no captions, no watermarks. The
  caption lives on the Canvas page, not in the pixels. Where a figure genuinely cannot work
  without a label, at most four short ones, and each prompt below says so explicitly.
- **Never generate a real product, a front panel, a named brand or a software window.**
  Those are captured or sourced. Part B lists the ones that need capturing.
- **Save generated figures as JPEG on their own background**, not transparent PNG.
- Target 1600 px on the long edge or larger.
