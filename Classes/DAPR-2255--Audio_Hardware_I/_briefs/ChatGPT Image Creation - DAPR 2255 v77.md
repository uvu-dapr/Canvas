## Check these two before you commit them

The two DMX images are the ones a generator is most likely to get wrong, because a connector face is
exactly the kind of detail it invents. Before you commit either one, open it and count.

| Image | The one thing to check |
|---|---|
| `midi-dmx-fixture-02.jpg` | **Count the contacts in each connector. There must be five.** Four is not a DMX connector and four is what came back last time. |
| `midi-dmx-terminator-02.jpg` | **Check that nothing leaves the back of the plug.** A terminator with a cable coming out of it is not a terminator, it is a cable. |

If either one comes back wrong a second time, photograph it instead. You own lighting gear, and a phone
photo of a real fixture on a sheet of white paper settles the pin count permanently. Standards 20.1
rule 3 says real hardware is not generated for exactly this reason, and these two connectors are the
clearest case of it in the whole course.
