Canvas Naming Scheme and Flexible Course Organization

A documentation standard for naming Canvas modules, pages, assignments, quizzes, files, and projects so content can move freely without being tied to a particular week.

Core principle: Canvas dates control the schedule. Names control meaning. Name content for what it is, what subject it belongs to, and where it fits in a learning sequence. Avoid permanent week-based names when the item may move during the semester.

What the Two Courses Show

Content first

Names identify the subject, task, project, skill, or assessment instead of relying on calendar position.

Sequence without weeks

Numbers such as M1, 01, Q1, 1.2, and 301 communicate sequence or level without saying "Week 4."

Reusable structure

Modules can be reordered, stretched, compressed, or copied into another semester without renaming every item.

Scales to complex courses

The same system works in a focused Spatial Audio course and in a much larger Networking, MIDI, and Electronics course.

Naming Patterns

Purpose Pattern Example
Topic or domain [Topic]: [Description] Networking: Ping
Module sequence [Topic]: M# - [Module Name] MIDI: M4 - MIDI Over USB and Network Systems
Numbered learning sequence [Topic]: ## [Description] Electronics: 03 AC & DC Circuits
Quiz tied to sequence or outcome [Topic]: Q# - [Description] Electronics: Q3 - CLO 3 - AC & DC Circuits
Multipart project [Project] #.# [Component] Orchestral Recording 1.3 Spatial Mix
Optional work [Normal Name] (Bonus) Wwise: 201 Quiz (Bonus)
Dated source artifact [Project]: YYYY-MM-DD [Artifact] Orchestral Recording 1: 2026-02-19 UVU Symphony Recording - Trimmed.zip
Scheduling rule: Use due dates, availability dates, prerequisites, requirements, and module order to control when students do the work. Use the title to explain what the work is.
Avoid: Week 3 Assignment, Week 6 Quiz, or Week 12 Atmos when the week is only a temporary scheduling decision. Those titles become wrong as soon as the course changes.

Quick Decision Test

Question Guidance
If I move this item three weeks later, will the title still be correct? If yes, the name is probably durable. If no, remove the temporary calendar reference.
Does the number communicate learning order, level, or project structure? Keep it. Meaningful sequence numbers are useful.
Does the date identify the actual recording, performance, or source artifact? Keep the date. That is identity, not scheduling.
Could the item appear in search, gradebook, copied courses, or exports without its module heading? Make the item name descriptive enough to stand on its own.
