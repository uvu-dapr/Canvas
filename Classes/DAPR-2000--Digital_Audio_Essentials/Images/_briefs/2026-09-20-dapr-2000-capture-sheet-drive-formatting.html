Capture sheet: drive formatting, Mac and Windows

For Sound: Read - Formatting a Drive That Works on Both Mac and Windows. Six screenshots. These are real OS dialogs, so per Standards section 20 they are captured, never generated. Take them on the machines the students actually use, so the version matches what they see.

Use a scratch drive. Every one of these shows a dialog that erases a disk. Plug in a stick with nothing on it, and never capture one of these with your real work drive selected, because the screenshot will show its name.

Lesson from the first batch. The Erase sheet arrived with the stick's existing volume name visible, and that name was a personal phone number. It appeared twice, in the sidebar and in the Name field. This repo is public. Before capturing an Erase dialog, rename the volume to something neutral, or expect the shot to be redacted afterward. The rule below about personal names was already here; this is what it looks like when it is missed.

Rules for all six

  • Destination folder: Classes/DAPR-2000--Digital_Audio_Essentials/Images/sound-the-decibel/
  • PNG, captured at the display's native resolution. Do not scale up afterward.
  • Crop to the dialog plus a little surrounding context. A full desktop screenshot makes the dialog unreadable at page width.
  • No personal file names, user names, or other drives visible in the sidebar.
  • Filenames exactly as listed. Do not rename.
