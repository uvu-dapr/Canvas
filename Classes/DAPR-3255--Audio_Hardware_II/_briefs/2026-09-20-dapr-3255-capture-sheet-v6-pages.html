Image Capture Sheet

DAPR 3255 Audio Hardware II, the fifteen pages added in v6. Eight captures. Everything here is real hardware or real software, so none of it is generated. Built 2026-09-20.

How to work this sheet

  • Work top to bottom. Each card names the exact filename and the exact folder.
  • Save the file under the name shown. Do not rename it afterwards.
  • Reload this page after each save. The preview slot fills in and prints the real pixel dimensions, and warns if the shot came in under 1600 px wide.
  • Empty slots are your to-do list.
  • Native resolution, cropped to the window. Never upscale.
  • Window captures only. No desktop, no Dock, no menu bar clutter, no other apps behind.
  • Nothing personal in frame. No student names, no email addresses, no visible path containing adamwolson, no notification banners. Do Not Disturb on first.
  • macOS light mode for any interface that offers it, so the shot does not become a black hole on a white Canvas page.
  • PNG for anything containing text or an interface. JPEG for a photograph of an object.
  • Manufacturer press photos are fine for gear not on hand. Prefer the manufacturer's own product image on white.
