PreSonus FaderPort Quick Start for Pro Tools

Every computer in our classrooms has a single-fader PreSonus FaderPort for simple automation and moving around a session. This guide covers what you need in Pro Tools. It is not every feature; the owner's manual below has the rest.

Step 1: Put the FaderPort in Pro Tools Mode

The FaderPort starts in Studio One mode. Pro Tools only talks to it in HUI mode, so set the mode first.

  1. Make sure the FaderPort's USB cable is plugged into the computer and its power supply is connected.
  2. Quit Pro Tools and turn the FaderPort off.
  3. Press and hold Next (the arrow button to the right of the blue knob) and turn the FaderPort on.
  4. Press Touch. This chooses Pro Tools (HUI) mode.
Skip this and the FaderPort will not work in Pro Tools.
If the fader and buttons do nothing in Pro Tools, quit Pro Tools and do Step 1 again.

Hold Next while you turn it on, then press Touch. Image: PreSonus FaderPort Owner's Manual, page 10.

Step 2: Set Up Pro Tools

  1. Open your Pro Tools session.
  2. Choose Setup > Peripherals and click the MIDI Controllers tab.
  3. On row #1, set Type to HUI.
  4. Set Receive From and Send To to PreSonus FP2. On some computers the port is named PreSonus FaderPort; choose that one.
  5. Set # Ch's to 8, then click OK.

Setup > Peripherals > MIDI Controllers: Type HUI, Receive From and Send To PreSonus FP2. Image: PreSonus FaderPort Owner's Manual, page 10.

Usually done for you
Pro Tools remembers this on each classroom computer. If row #1 already shows HUI and the FaderPort port, just click OK.

Getting Around the Session

The fader controls the selected track. The buttons around the blue knob choose what the knob and the Prev and Next buttons do.

The session navigator. Image: PreSonus FaderPort Owner's Manual, page 12.

What the FaderPort buttons do in Pro Tools
ButtonIn Pro Tools
ChannelThe knob, Prev and Next pick the track the fader controls. SHIFT + Channel locks the fader to that track.
PanThe knob pans the selected track. SHIFT + Pan puts panning on the fader.
ScrollThe knob scrubs audio; press twice for faster scrubbing. SHIFT + Scroll zooms instead. Press Stop to end it.
MarkerPress the knob to drop a marker. The knob, Prev and Next move between markers.
ClickTurns the metronome on and off. It only works if the session has a click track.
Solo, Mute, ArmSolo, mute or record-arm the selected track. SHIFT + Arm arms every track.
SHIFT + Prev / NextUndo / Redo.
Stop, Play, RecordTransport. Press Record and then Play to record on armed tracks.
Rewind and Fast ForwardMove back or forward. Press both at once to return to the start of the session.
Link, Master, Section, BypassDo nothing in Pro Tools.

Write Automation with the Fader

The automation buttons. Image: PreSonus FaderPort Owner's Manual, page 12.

  1. Press Channel and turn the knob to select the track.
  2. Press Touch. The track writes automation only while your finger is on the fader. (SHIFT + Touch is Latch: it keeps writing after you let go, until you stop.)
  3. Press Play and move the fader. The fader is touch-sensitive, so just touching it starts the write.
  4. Press Stop, then press Read to play your moves back. The motorized fader moves on its own.
Write replaces everything it passes over.
Write records the fader for the whole pass, even where you do not move it. Use Touch for small fixes, and switch back to Read when you are done.

Quick Fixes

Common FaderPort problems and what to check
ProblemWhat to check
Nothing happens in Pro ToolsThe FaderPort is not in HUI mode: quit Pro Tools and do Step 1 again. Then check Setup > Peripherals (Step 2).
The FaderPort is not in the Receive From listCheck the USB cable and the power supply, turn the FaderPort on, then quit and reopen Pro Tools.
The fader moves the wrong trackPress Channel and pick the track with the knob, or SHIFT + Channel to lock it.
Click does nothingCreate a click track first (Track > Create Click Track).
The fader moves by itselfThe track is in Read and has automation. That is normal.
