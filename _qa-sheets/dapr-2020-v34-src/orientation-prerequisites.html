Course Description

Teaches the science and art of audio mixing, centering on a broad range of musical and media post-production material. Covers initial plan, signal flow, and skills for all signal processors, including spectral, dynamic, time-based and distortion effects. Explores audio editing techniques. Also teaches mix room acoustics, treatments and workarounds.

Why Core Mixing comes before Core Recording right now. DAPR 2020: Core Mixing was moved to fall semester to open up space in the studio, which means it currently runs ahead of DAPR 2010: Core Recording. That is a scheduling decision about room availability and nothing else.

The intended sequence is the other way round: DAPR 2010: Core Recording in the fall alongside DAPR 2010L: Core Recording Lab, then DAPR 2020: Core Mixing in the spring alongside DAPR 2020L: Core Mixing Lab. You are not missing anything by taking them in this order, and the catalog prerequisite is unchanged.
