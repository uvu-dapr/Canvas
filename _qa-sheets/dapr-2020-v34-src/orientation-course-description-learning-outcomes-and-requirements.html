The arc of the course runs from the room you listen in, through the performance, to the tools you shape it with. The six things you will be able to do by the end are below, and every module attaches to at least one of them.

1

The Room

Identify potential acoustic problems within the mixing space.

2

The Take

Explain how to comp multiple takes into a cohesive performance.

3

The Motion

Describe how to generate forward motion in an arrangement.

4

The Repair

Investigate distracting timing and tuning problems in a mix.

5

The Discipline

Distinguish between professional and amateur signal flow and session organization.

6

The Tools

Explain spectral, dynamic, distortion, and time-based effects in the mixing process.
